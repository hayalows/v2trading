How to use this

Stage 0–2 means there is little to watch. Stage 3–4 means a sequence is developing. Stage 5–6 means structure exists and the chart deserves attention. Stage 7 means price is near the POI. Stage 8 means the research entry area has been reached, not that you should trade.

Stage guide

What each part of the V2 sequence means in plain English

0–2 · Ignore / earlyNo usable setup yet, price merely near liquidity, or the POI has already been used.
3–4 · DevelopingA liquidity sweep exists. The key question is whether a valid break of structure follows.
5–6 · Structure existsBOS has occurred and the engine is identifying or tracking a fresh point of interest.
7–8 · Close watchPrice is approaching or has reached the research entry zone. Still not a trade instruction.
Can it find setups before entry?

A strict candle-by-candle historical replay is being used to test this exact question. The test hides every future candle and measures whether Stage 3+ appears before Stage 8.

Can it tell whether the trade will win?

No. That remains blocked by the execution-label problem found in v0.4. This version is a formation detector and market-analysis lab, not a proven trading signal engine.

Gold remains experimental

I have not deleted the gold research. It is removed from the automatic core scan because spot and futures data do not share one clean execution price. We can bring XAUUSD back as a dedicated gold lab once we have a better free data design.

Research only. The public price feed is not broker execution truth. The most useful way to use this version is to compare what the detector says with what you can see on the chart, then let the timestamped prospective dataset accumulate.
