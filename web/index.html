POI midpoint → sweep stop → 2.5R

Entry remains the 50% POI midpoint on a future completed M15 bar, SL remains beyond the sweep extreme with a 0.03 ATR buffer, and TP remains fixed at 2.5R. What changed is the waiting rule: eight bars is now a lifecycle milestone, not an expiry.

hourglass_topPOI waiting-time research

The old eight-bar cutoff was too early to call a POI dead.

The public proxy found a 31.1% midpoint fill rate by 8 bars, 53.6% by 24 bars, 65.3% by 48 bars and 82.5% by the 192-bar research tail. These are descriptive public-proxy fill frequencies, not a probability forecast for the current trade.

lockIndependence gate

The independent prospective sample is still building.

A denominator is misleading when several observations come from overlapping sweeps in the same market campaign. The lab compresses those correlated sweep events before any live percentage can unlock.

fact_checkHistorical detector validation

Formation discovery passed; profitability did not.

In causal replay, future candles were hidden across 154,665 M15 bars. Against 279 independently generated proxy entries, Stage 5 appeared beforehand 82.1% of the time and Stage 6 71.3%, typically around one hour before entry. This is recall, not win rate.

menu_bookQuick glossary
Formation campaignexpand_more

One continuous same-direction Stage-3+ market sequence. It starts after the structure becomes meaningful and ends on reset or direction change. A campaign may contain several distinct sweep events.

Fresh POIexpand_more

The origin zone identified only after break of structure confirms. Once a fresh zone exists, POI Watch shows its high, low, distance and current relationship to structure price.

POI lifecycleexpand_more

Fresh wait covers the first eight M15 bars, extended wait continues through 48 bars, long-tail wait continues through the 192-bar research tail, and older untouched plans remain tracked as outside studied tail. Time alone is not called invalidation.

Partial mitigationexpand_more

Price touches part of the POI but does not reach the midpoint entry. Historical proxy outcomes were materially weaker after this pattern, so the lab downgrades the context but continues tracking it.

Pre-entry extensionexpand_more

How far price moved in the intended direction before the midpoint entry was reached. A prior 2.5R extension is recorded as context, not automatically treated as cancellation.

Automatic paper tradeexpand_more

A simulated research trade created automatically from the lab's POI midpoint rule. It records the planned entry, stop, target, waiting lifecycle, actual bar-based entry event and outcome without requiring a manual journal entry.

Research vs execution truthexpand_more

Paper trades use public completed structural bars. They do not represent the price, spread, slippage or fill sequence your broker would have produced.
