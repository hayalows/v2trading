Model decisions

Strict price/setup model
Accepted research baseline

Completed-bar-only market state still ranks V2-like setups out of sample. It remains research-only because the execution labels are not yet robust.

Economic-event model
Rejected

A causal event model did not generalize. The fixed 80/20 price+event blend reduced pooled AUC from 0.713 to 0.707.

XAU macro/geopolitical model
Rejected

2025 was promising, but the overall blend worsened AUC/calibration and 2024 was near random. No post-hoc rescue or inversion.

Live trading
Disabled

The research model may rank historical setups. It is not authorized to produce current buy/sell recommendations until execution validation passes.

What v0.3 changed

1 · Causal price stateNo eventual entry-candle close or volume.
2 · Causal eventsFuture releases expose schedule only, not future surprise.
3 · Lagged gold macroYields, USD, VIX, oil and GPR are delayed before use.
4 · M1/tick attackIndependent path and bid/ask replay test the labels themselves.
5 · Execution-first nextRebuild labels around fillability and spread/risk before adding more AI.
Research verdict: v0.3 supports continuing the project, but not trading it. The strongest finding is a timestamp-clean setup-ranking signal. The weakest link is execution: independent M1 and bid/ask data disagree too often with the proxy outcome labels, and transaction friction is large relative to many planned stops.
