How to use the lab

Background: little reason to focus. Watchlist: sweep/development exists, but many die. Review now: BOS / fresh POI exists and deserves chart inspection. Location: price is near or inside the research zone.

Quant-research rule: context can support or conflict with a formation, but it does not override the deterministic V2 state machine. New models must earn their place out of sample.
