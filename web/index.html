menu_bookQuick glossary
Formation campaignexpand_more

One continuous same-direction Stage-3+ market sequence. It starts after the structure becomes meaningful and ends on reset or direction change. A campaign may contain several distinct sweep events.

Sweep eventexpand_more

A specific liquidity sweep detected inside a campaign. Every sweep is retained for research, but repeated sweeps inside one campaign do not each count as independent evidence.

Fresh POIexpand_more

The origin zone identified only after break of structure confirms. The app deliberately shows no POI before BOS. Once a fresh zone exists, POI Watch shows its high, low, distance and current relationship to structure price.

Research priorityexpand_more

An action category based on structural maturity, data status and market context: No focus, Observe, Observe counter-context, Review structure, Investigate location, Data hold, or Review only. It is not a trade recommendation.

Independent evidence gateexpand_more

The lab withholds prospective outcome percentages until enough completed formation campaigns exist. Raw sweep outcomes are shown as diagnostics but do not control the inference gate.

MFE / MAEexpand_more

Maximum favorable and adverse excursion after an anchor, normalized by ATR. These describe price movement and do not include broker spread, slippage or fill logic.
