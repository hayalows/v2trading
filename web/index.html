How to read the stages

Validated UX: sensitivity first, selectivity later

0–2 · BackgroundNo mature structure. Usually no reason to focus on this pair yet.
3–4 · WatchlistSweep/development. These stages caught almost every later proxy entry, but most early sequences did not complete. Watch, do not assume.
5–6 · Review nowBOS is confirmed; Stage 6 also has a fresh POI. About 71% of independent proxy entries had reached Stage 6 beforehand, usually about 60 minutes early.
7–8 · LocationPrice is near or inside the research zone. Stage 7 was not required for most proxy entries, so treat it as a proximity flag, not a quality score.

Can it tell which trade will win?

No. v0.6 validates formation discovery, not profitability. The v0.4 execution-label failure still blocks buy/sell signals and win probabilities. The right use today is: discover → inspect → record → research.
