menu_bookQuick glossary
Formation episodeexpand_more

One continuous same-direction Stage-3+ sequence. It starts once, can mature through later stages, and ends on reset or direction change. Five-minute refreshes do not create five separate opportunities.

Research priorityexpand_more

An action category based on structural maturity, data status and market state: No focus, Observe, Review structure, Investigate location, Data hold, or Review only. It is not a trade recommendation.

Evidence gateexpand_more

The lab withholds prospective outcome percentages until enough completed independent episodes exist. This prevents one or two unusual cases from becoming false certainty.

MFE / MAEexpand_more

Maximum favorable and adverse excursion after an episode anchor, normalized by ATR. These describe price movement and do not include broker spread, slippage or fill logic.
