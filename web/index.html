Quick glossary

Stageexpand_more

Where the current V2-like formation sits in the structural sequence. Higher stages mean more of the sequence has occurred; they do not mean a higher probability of profit.

Context fitexpand_more

Whether the weighted D1, H4, H1, and M15 direction supports, conflicts with, or is mixed relative to the current formation direction.

Shift riskexpand_more

A descriptive measure of unusual change pressure. Elevated or high states were historically followed by larger absolute movement, but the metric does not predict direction.

Directional efficiencyexpand_more

How directly price travelled over a recent window. Higher efficiency means a cleaner directional path; lower efficiency means more back-and-forth noise.
